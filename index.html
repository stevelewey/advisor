IV. Leadership Advisor

A Confidential Thinking Partner for the Weight You're Carrying

Some decisions are too important to make alone, and too personal to bring to just anyone. This is a confidential place to think clearly, be honest, and get wisdom you can actually use — before Monday.

I work with business owners, leaders, planters, and shepherds who are navigating something big — a hard decision, a season of transition, a team or ministry that's outgrown their current tools for leading it. This isn't therapy, and it isn't consulting in the traditional sense. It's an advisory relationship, a confidential thinking partnership, drawn from Proverbs and tested across decades in the real work of marketplace and ministry.

There are two ways to work together, depending on what you need right now.

Who This Is For

  • You lead or own a business, ministry, or team, and people depend on the decisions you make.
  • You're facing something — a decision, a transition, a conflict — that's hard to talk through with employees, customers, or family.
  • You want a sounding board with real experience, not a script or a certification.
  • You want your faith and your leadership to actually integrate, not sit in separate compartments.

What Happens in a Session

BEFORE

You come with the real issue

No agenda-building or small talk to wade through — we start with whatever's actually weighing on you.

DURING

We think it through together

The issue gets examined from more than one angle, with honest pushback where it's needed, not just agreement.

AFTER

You leave with clarity

Every session ends with the core issue named plainly and a decision or next step you can actually act on.

A Few Things Worth Knowing

How do sessions get booked?

Once your application is approved, booking is confirmed with a deposit. This holds your time and reflects that both of us are serious about the work ahead.

What's the cancellation policy?

Life happens, so reschedule with as much notice as you can. Deposits are non-refundable for cancellations made within 24 hours of a scheduled session or call.

How long does it take to hear back after I apply?

I read every application personally. Expect a reply within a few business days, either way.
